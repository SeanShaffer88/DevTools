# Learning roadmaps and deep references

For building real understanding — an AI can use these to fill gaps instead of guessing, and humans can follow them as curricula.

**10 resources.**

- **[Karpathy — Neural Networks: Zero to Hero](https://github.com/karpathy/nanoGPT)** — _Repo / course._ nanoGPT: the simplest readable GPT training implementation; pair with the YouTube series of the same name.
- **[Karpathy — llm.c](https://github.com/karpathy/llm.c)** — _Repo._ GPT training in plain C/CUDA — what frameworks abstract away, made visible.
- **[Hugging Face LLM Course](https://huggingface.co/learn/llm-course)** — _Course._ Free course: Transformers, fine-tuning, datasets, sharing models.
- **[Microsoft — AI for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/AI-For-Beginners)** — _Repo / course._ 12-week curriculum: neural networks, CV, NLP, ethics.
- **[Microsoft — Generative AI for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/generative-ai-for-beginners)** — _Repo / course._ 21-lesson course on building GenAI apps (Python/TypeScript/.NET tracks).
- **[Microsoft — ML for Beginners](https://github.com/microsoft/ML-For-Beginners)** — _Repo / course._ Classical ML curriculum with projects.
- **[LLM Course (mlabonne)](https://github.com/mlabonne/llm-course)** — _Repo / roadmap._ Roadmap with notebooks: LLM fundamentals, scientist, and engineer tracks.
- **[The Illustrated Transformer](https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/)** — _Article._ Jay Alammar's visual walkthrough of the Transformer architecture.
- **[Fast.ai — Practical Deep Learning](https://course.fast.ai/)** — _Course._ Top-down deep learning course (free).
- **[DeepLearning.AI short courses](https://www.deeplearning.ai/courses)** — _Course index._ Short practitioner courses on RAG, agents, fine-tuning, and evals.
